# Hunger of the Constant

The food from the Don't Starve series, in Minecraft! Prepare food in a Crock Pot
and come across new resources in the wild.

## Features

- Fully data-driven, custom cooking ingredients and recipes can be
  created/tweaked with datapacks.
- Obtain new resources from mob loot and world generation.
- Extend the hunger value of some food items or lower some for status effects
  by cooking them into prepared food items.
- Get 10% to 40% more saturation from prepared food items compared to vanilla 
  food items.
- Make food for no fuel, for the cost of longer cooking times.
- Mix inedible items into edible food.
- Act like these games actually did a crossover.
